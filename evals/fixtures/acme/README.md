# Acme fixture

Illustrative. Acme is an invented company: invoicing software for trades businesses, Series C, about 400 people in 12 squads, with an AI assistant that drafts quotes and chases invoices. Every figure, bet, owner and reading here is made up for the tests. Owners are roles, not people.

- `bets.csv`, `work.csv`, `readings.csv`: the CSV format, one bet per gap the review must catch.
- `linear-projects.json`, `linear-issues.json`: the same idea in the shape the Linear connector returns.
- `jira-export.csv`: the same idea as a Jira CSV export (epics are bets).
